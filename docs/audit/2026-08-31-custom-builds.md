## Top 5 replacements by lines-of-code deleted

1. **`science/collect.py` → `duckdb.read_json_auto`** — ~300 lines deleted. Verdict already reached by the estate's own `duckdb_differential.py` (crew#74, crew#104); a proof drill exists and the cutover just hasn't happened.
2. **26 gate/guard scripts → OPA/conftest for the ~15 static-file predicates** — roughly 1,200–1,500 of the 2,721 lines are pure "read a file, assert a predicate" checks (`actions-pinned`, `port-gate`, `plist-gate`, `static-secret-gate`, `ns-fence-gate`, `owner-account-gate`, `multiarch-gate`, `spec-gate`, `matrix-gate`, `policy-test`, `idp-envsubst-gate`, `idp-verdict-fresh`, `cloud-agnostic-gate`, `catalog-refcheck`, `catalog-links-check`) that Rego/conftest expresses in far fewer lines each.
3. **`crew/crew/cli.py` → crewAI Task/Process/memory (543 lines)** — the entire plan/claim/evidence/verify loop is a candidate for folding into the orchestration framework already being adopted; largest single-file fold in scope.
4. **9 litellm/langfuse/mcp up/down/status scripts → one generic compose wrapper (858 lines)** — three near-identical triads collapse to one parameterised script, likely under 150 lines total.
5. **`science/foresight.py` model-tracking half → MLflow** — the logistic-regression train/predict/score loop hand-rolls its own model persistence and scoring ledger where MLflow (already a platform layer, currently unused by this file) does exactly this; savings not measured (file not opened past line 15) but flagged as the clearest platform-layer-sitting-idle-while-a-script-reinvents-it case.
