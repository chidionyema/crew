Founder, 2026-08-29: *"the research capability is useless"* — *"they should have generated
thousands of ideas ready for the store front"* — *"while we were firefighting, they had all the
data to track the actual bottleneck with real numbers and help us address it"* — *"they are a bit
of a black box"* — *"it needs to be a general purpose engine that can be pointed at anything and
researches thoroughly, serving all parts of the estate including prospector, same with all

Every number below was measured on 2026-08-29 from `chidionyema/crew` origin/main and from this
machine. The command that produces each one is named beside it.

## The verdict in one line

The department is not underperforming at the job you want. **It was never given that job**, and
right now it is not running at all.

## Nothing watches the outside world

You said we should already have a comprehensive view of the tech affecting our stack. Searching
the repo for any file named for watching upstream tech — a radar, a vendor watch, a release-notes

## Why. This is the part that matters.

`roles/science.md`, the chief scientist's charter, in full at the top:

> You do not chat with the founder. You talk to the issue.
>
> You own two questions and nothing else:
>
> 1. Does the estate know what is true? Every number it acts on, and how wrong it is.
> 2. Can it tell what caused what? Attribution, before any repair claims a cause.
>
> You do not own building, shipping, uptime or cost.

Both questions point **inward**, at our own numbers. Nothing about markets, competitors, tech,
products, prospector, marketing or sales. And the first line is *"You do not chat with the
founder"* — the exact opposite of a crew member who owns a lane and consults with you.

`docs/research-engine/CHARTER.md` says the same thing in its charge: *"Make every product on the
platform provably right and measurably improving."* Verification, not generation.

So: the department was chartered as an internal auditor of our own arithmetic. It has been doing
that job. The outward-facing, general-purpose research engine you are describing was never
created, and the scoreboard then hid it — `RESEARCH-GRADE.md` grades the lane **ELITE** on "30 of
30 questions fed a decision", because it counts questions and has no row for ideas, for tech
watched, or for anything a buyer would pay for. A lane can grade ELITE forever while producing
one idea.

## The one answer

Do not repair the science lane. It is doing its chartered job and that job is worth keeping —
somebody has to check our own numbers, and that is what caught the false claims today.

Create the thing that is missing: **one general-purpose research engine, pointable at any target,
producing a sourced dossier and a falsifiable claim, running on a schedule nobody starts.** The
science lane stays as its grader — it scores the engine's claims, which is exactly what its
charter is for. Prospector is the proof this works: it is pointed at a target, it runs by itself,
it produces content, and it was ducktaped together. This one gets built properly.

Targets on day one: our own stack's upstream tech, prospector's market, marketing, sales, product
development. The same engine, a different target, one ledger.

## Who owns it

It needs a named crew member whose charter says the opposite of the science charter: outward,
generative, and it consults with you. That is a new role file, not an edit to `roles/science.md`
— and naming that person is your call, not mine.

## The shape it has to take

Founder, 2026-08-29: *"that's why we build capabilities, so any agent or human or machine can
utilise the capabilities."*

This is the constraint that decides the build, and it is the one the current department fails
hardest. Its 23 capabilities are scripts in one repo's `science/` folder. To use one you must know
it exists, have the repo checked out, and type its command. No other agent can call it. Prospector
cannot call it. You cannot call it from your phone. That is why the department is a black box —
not because it hides anything, but because there is no door.

The research engine is a capability with one door, and three kinds of caller come through it:

- **an agent** — a tool it can call, listed with the rest, no checkout and no command to remember
- **a human** — one phrase to you, or one entry on a page, that names a target and gets a dossier
- **a machine** — a schedule or another service asking without anybody present

Same engine, same ledger, same quality contract behind all three. It gets a catalogue entity like
every other service on the platform, so it is discoverable rather than remembered. If it only
works when a session types a command in one repo, it has been built wrong again.
