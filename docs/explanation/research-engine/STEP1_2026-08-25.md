## Baseline: old prospector dossier 79e36b3878b6d081 (2026-08-14)
Placed here for side-by-side only (ruling R32). It was not an input to the engine.
- Title: FTSA comp verdicts for Florida AI voice vendors
- One-liner: Comparable verdicts from Florida Telephone Solicitation Act cases against outbound AI callers, so a founder-operator knows what their call pattern has historically cost defendants.
- Hypothesis (one, given to it): Florida Statute 501.059, the Telephone Solicitation Act, gives every Florida resident a private right of action for unsolicited telephone calls and prerecorded messages, with statutory damages of $500 to $1,500 per call. AI-generated voice calls fall within the statute's plain text. Cases are filed in the county court where the plaintiff lives, and the settlements, verdicts and amounts paid sit in 67 separate county Clerk of Court dockets.

AI voice startups running outbound into Florida have no way to price their exposure because no aggregated record exists. This vertical tool scrapes the FL Clerk dockets, classifies each case by call pattern (predictive dialer, AI voice, prerecorded, SMS),...
- Decision: kill (min_composite); composite 1.85 vs bar 2.5
- Sources: 65
- Model: fallback(claude_cli+standardcompute+minimax)
- Scores: pain_acuity 2, money_provability 1, automatability 3, distribution 1, defensibility 2, build_feasibility 3

What it did: took one idea someone fed it, ran six checks, killed it. It generated no hypothesis of its own, designed no experiment, stated no prior, and cannot say what would change its mind.
