# R73 — Business people do not use the command line: eject from the terminal harness

**Founder, 2026-09-01 ~21:00Z, verbatim record:**
`docs/founder/2026-09-01T2100Z-eject-from-the-harness-business-interfaces.md` (this repo).

## The ruling

1. **The terminal is a workshop tool, never the founder's surface.** Business conversations —
   direction, priorities, decisions, daily reports — happen on human-led interfaces: visual
   pages, voice, connected apps. Seamless. "BUSINESS PEOPLE DO NOT USE COMMAND LINE."
2. **Cryptic talk to the founder is a defect, every time.** Check names, branch names, hashes,
   tool words — none of it belongs in a business meeting. Even in developer work, clear
   communication is preferred. This has been ruled repeatedly (plain-English rulings of
   2026-08-29 and 2026-08-31); it now carries the on-the-spot correction rule of R71.
3. **The strategy gap is the priority.** "Not even 0.001% of what needs to be strategised" is
   done. The CEO owns the interface roadmap: how the founder meets the company — reads reports,
   gives direction, approves decisions — without ever opening a terminal. Claude Code and every
   other developer harness stay in the workshop, driven by agents, not by him.
4. **Prove it in our own company first** (R71 tenet): the first deliverable is the founder's
   own daily surface — visual, plain-language, one place — with voice and chat following.
