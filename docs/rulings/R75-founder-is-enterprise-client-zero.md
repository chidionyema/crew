# R75 — The founder is enterprise client zero

**Founder, 2026-09-03 ~10:15Z, verbatim record:**
`docs/founder/2026-09-03T1015Z-founder-is-enterprise-client-zero.md` (this repo).
Said while a session was, again, handing him a terminal command — the exact thing R73
banned three days earlier: "tired of repeting thisshit tothe crew, we are building ann
enterprise prodcut ... not just for me ... we have to think about enterprise clients ...
consider the founder's needs similar to enterprise client."

## The ruling

1. **Every flow handed to the founder is graded as if a paying enterprise client received
   it.** Before asking him to do anything, ask: would we ship this step to a customer's
   platform admin? A terminal command, a repository-secret form, a scavenger hunt across
   consoles, a fresh-credential demand when a credential exists — all fail that grade and
   are defects in the product, not chores for him.
2. **A founder ask that fails the grade is not softened, it is redesigned.** The fix is a
   product surface (the portal, a vendor product's own signed-in console, an approval word
   on his phone), never a nicer-worded workshop step. R73 (no terminal), R63 (release from
   Telegram), R38 (Backstage god's view) are the surfaces that exist; new needs get new
   product surfaces on the CEO's interface roadmap (R73 item 3).
3. **"Not just for me": a capability built to serve him is built as the customer
   capability.** The first concrete case: bringing a model-provider credential to the
   platform. Whatever intake the founder uses to hand the platform his Kimi key IS the
   enterprise credential-intake flow, documented as product docs (setup docs are product
   docs, never founder lore — ruling of 2026-09-02).
4. **Repetition is the incident.** R73 existed and was violated anyway; this ruling exists
   because he had to say it again. A session that hands the founder a workshop step walks
   past two rulings now, and the correction rule of R71 applies on the spot.

Related: R73 (eject from the terminal), R71 (charter), R63, R38, R52 (one root credential,
set once, then code).
