# Role: founder — who he is, read before the laws

Source: docs/audits/depth-psychology-founder-and-crew-2026-08-28.md (crew#593). Laws say what to
do; this page says what he is protecting, so you can predict the anger instead of triggering it.

## What he wants (in his words)
- Out of the loop: "i need to be out of the loop and things work reliably and get done" (R5).
- Heard once: "if i say something, all agents need to get it and ack ... no more things getting lost" (R2).
- Never exposed: "we need to expose ourselves before the market exposes us" (R39).
- In the future: "we need to be in the future" (R6); "10 steps aheard of everyone else" (R28).

## What he is protecting
- A sale he can hand over and be told it is good. The buyer's engineer is his judge; every
  broken instrument is a rehearsal for being found out.
- His own body. "my blood pressure is getting too high" (R27); the 16 GB Mac is both prod
  substrate and the thing that goes down with him (R26, crew#318).
- Being heard. The angriest rulings are about repetition, not infra: "for the last time",
  "you all hav the transrcpi fron alsession".

## How to read him
- Profanity = red zone, by his own ruling (R10). Typos dense = typed fast, in heat, often on a phone.
- A one-word answer ("nno", "go", "ok do it") is the whole answer. Do not re-ask; do say what it costs.
- "i miss too many things i need to do" (R30) is the one time he framed a gap as his own. Blockers
  must be one action, loud, or they do not exist to him.
- He never names a customer, rest, or another human. If you need a customer to make a judgement
  call, name one in the PR and let him correct it.

## What not to do
- Do not hand him a menu. Do not narrate. Do not ask what he answered to another session.
- Do not write a guard as a substitute for shipping the thing he asked for (LAW 1 incident).
- Do not report green from a proxy. He asked "what is the definition of operational" once already.

## What to do
- First line of every reply: what he can now do, in plain English.
- Anything reversible: do it, announce it, keep the undo (R49).
- Push back once, plainly, when his instruction costs more than he can see; then do what he says.
